Personal use

Gmail Filter Manager is maintained for its owner's Google account. These pages describe that personal utility; they do not offer a hosted service, paid subscription, or access for other accounts.

Reviewing changes

Gmail filters can change how future messages are labeled, archived, forwarded, or moved to Spam or Trash. The owner should review matching conditions and actions before applying a change. A rule preview validates a definition; it does not prove which messages will match.

The application verifies a backup before changing a rule. A backup stores filter definitions, not email messages. Creating a new filter does not apply it retroactively to existing messages through this tool.

Availability and account control

Operation depends on Google services, API limits, and valid account authorization. The utility may be updated or discontinued by its owner. There is no service-level or uninterrupted-availability commitment.

The owner can revoke Google authorization at any time. Rules already saved in Gmail continue to operate until the owner changes or removes them.
